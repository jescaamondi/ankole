The Ankole Grill Story

Our name comes from the Ankole Watusi, the long-horned cattle of East and Central Africa. For the Nilotic peoples of the region, cattle have always meant more than food. They stand for family, wealth and community.

Ankole Grill brings that spirit to the table. Pull up a chair for a warm, generous meal in the tradition of African hospitality.
